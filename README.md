## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science undergraduate at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG)** with a technical background in Systems Development and experience in academic research and software engineering.

My main interest is building intelligent systems that operate under real-world constraints.

At UFMG's **Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence Research Laboratory**, I worked with machine learning, computer networks, time-series representations and **TinyML**, including experiments on resource-constrained hardware such as microcontrollers and edge devices.

That experience shaped the direction I want to pursue:
